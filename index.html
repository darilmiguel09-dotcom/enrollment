About Our School

Taal Junior and Senior High School provides junior and senior high school education that helps students develop knowledge and skills for college, their future careers and life.

Our Mission

To provide quality basic education which will serve as their weapon and foundation for a life-long learnings and service for the common good.

Our Vision

Taal High School is an educational institution committed to discover full potential of every youth towards becoming responsible and productive members of society who are maka-Diyos, maka-Tao, maka-Kalikasan and maka-Bayan.
